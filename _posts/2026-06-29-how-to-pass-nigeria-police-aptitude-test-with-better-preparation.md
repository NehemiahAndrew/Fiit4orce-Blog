If you want to pass the Nigeria Police aptitude test, preparation should be steady and practical. Many candidates spend too much time searching for shortcuts and too little time building real exam readiness.

A stronger routine beats panic every time.

## What should you study most?

Build your preparation around:

- English comprehension
- mathematics and quantitative reasoning
- current affairs
- general knowledge
- logic and time-based question practice

Use practice sessions to improve both accuracy and speed. The test is not only about knowing answers. It is also about managing time well.

## How should you use practice questions?

Practice questions are most helpful when you review mistakes carefully. After every session, identify the questions you missed, why you missed them, and what topic needs more attention.

That review step is where many candidates improve.

## What mistakes should Police candidates avoid?

Avoid studying only your favorite subject. Avoid depending on fake answer leaks. Avoid ignoring timing until the final week. Also avoid relying on unofficial claims about the exact test format unless they match the current public guidance.
