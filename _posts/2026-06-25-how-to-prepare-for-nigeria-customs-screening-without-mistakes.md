If you want to prepare for Nigeria Customs screening without mistakes, the best approach is to start early, verify your instructions from the official source, and avoid last-minute confusion. Many screening problems happen because candidates rush their document checks, rely on rumor, or underestimate how demanding the day can feel.

A calm and organized plan makes a big difference.

## What screening preparation usually involves

Nigeria Customs screening preparation is usually more than simply showing up. Candidates often need to be ready for instructions, verification, waiting, movement, and formal checks.

That means strong preparation should focus on:

- official guidance
- document organization
- timing and movement
- physical freshness
- mental calm

## Verify the official instructions first

Before you trust any screening checklist, go back to the official recruitment source and confirm the latest guidance yourself.

Use the official source to check:

- current screening notices
- reporting instructions
- document guidance
- any update linked to your stage or center

If a message conflicts with the official source, trust the official source.

## Organize your documents early

One of the most common screening mistakes is waiting too long to organize materials. That increases stress and makes simple errors more likely.

A safer habit is to:

- gather all required materials early
- make sure everything is readable
- separate items clearly if needed
- keep them together in one accessible folder

Late-night scrambling usually creates more confusion.

## Plan your movement and timing

Do not assume screening will be quick or simple. Travel delays, uncertainty, and crowd pressure can make the day harder than expected.

A better plan is to:

- know your route beforehand
- leave enough time for delays
- avoid arriving tired or confused
- prepare for the process to take time

## Stay physically ready without overtraining

Last-minute extreme workouts often backfire. You want freshness, not burnout.

In the days before screening:

- sleep well
- stay hydrated
- keep active without pushing too hard
- avoid crash dieting
- stay steady rather than desperate
