If you want to prepare for the Nigerian Navy aptitude test, start with a simple rule: build a steady study routine before pressure arrives. Many candidates wait until screening is close, then try to cover too much at once. That usually leads to stress, weak retention, and careless mistakes.

The better approach is to prepare in small daily blocks and verify every current instruction from the official Navy recruitment source.

## What should you study first?

Most aptitude preparation should begin with the basics:

- English comprehension
- mathematics and quantitative reasoning
- current affairs
- general knowledge
- simple logic and speed drills

Do not only read. Practice under time. The aptitude test rewards accuracy, but it also rewards candidates who can think clearly without wasting minutes.

## How should you use past questions?

Past questions are useful when you treat them as training, not as magic predictions. Use them to understand question style, common weak areas, and timing. After each session, review the questions you missed and write down why you missed them.

That review habit is where real improvement happens.

## What mistakes should Navy candidates avoid?

Avoid relying on random WhatsApp files, fake answer leaks, or unofficial claims about exact questions. These can distract you from genuine preparation. Also avoid studying only one subject because it feels comfortable.

A balanced routine is safer.
