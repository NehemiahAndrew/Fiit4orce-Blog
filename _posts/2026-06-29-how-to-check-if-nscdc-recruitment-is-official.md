To check if NSCDC recruitment is official, verify the information from a real government or NSCDC-linked source before you click, pay, upload documents, or share personal details. Recruitment scams often look urgent, polished, and convincing, but that does not make them genuine.

The safest habit is to pause and verify.

## What does official NSCDC recruitment information usually include?

Official recruitment information should clearly point candidates to recognized channels, public instructions, and a process that can be checked without private negotiation. It should not depend on a personal phone number, secret form, or payment demand.

When reviewing a message, ask:

- Does it point to an official source?
- Does the process match public instructions?
- Is anyone asking for money or private help?
- Is the link suspicious or shortened?

If the answer feels wrong, do not proceed.

## What are common NSCDC scam signs?

Be careful when a message promises guaranteed selection, asks for processing fees, creates panic, or tells you to ignore official channels. Scammers often use pressure because they do not want candidates to think clearly.

Official recruitment should not require blind trust in a stranger.
