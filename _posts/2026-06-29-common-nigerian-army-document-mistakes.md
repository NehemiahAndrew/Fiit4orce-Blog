Common Nigerian Army document mistakes can hurt candidates who are otherwise serious about recruitment. Many problems are avoidable: missing photocopies, unclear printouts, name mismatches, late preparation, or trusting the wrong checklist.

Documents may look simple, but they deserve attention.

## What document mistakes happen often?

Candidates should watch out for:

- spelling differences across documents
- unclear photocopies
- missing application printouts
- damaged certificates
- forgotten passport photographs
- relying on old screening lists
- mixing originals and copies carelessly

These issues can create stress during screening.

## How should you prepare your documents?

Start early. Keep one folder for originals and another for copies. Review names, dates, and document quality. If the official notice asks for a specific item, move it to the front of your checklist so it is not forgotten.

Do not wait until the night before screening to arrange everything.

## Why official instructions matter

Different recruitment cycles may request different items. A checklist from an old intake can be useful for early planning, but it should never replace the current official instruction.

Official guidance is the final authority.
