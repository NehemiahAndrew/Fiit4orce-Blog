Yes, genuine Nigeria Customs recruitment should be treated as free unless an official notice clearly states otherwise through the proper official channel. In practice, random payment requests are one of the clearest signs that a candidate may be dealing with a scam.

That is why you should become extremely careful once money enters the conversation.

## Why this question matters

Scammers often target candidates by claiming there is a fee for registration, shortlist placement, screening access, or final selection. These claims usually sound urgent and designed to create panic.

That pressure is exactly the point.

## Fake payment claims to watch for

Be careful if anyone says you must pay for:

- application access
- placement on a shortlist
- screening confirmation
- insider processing
- guaranteed success

Those are major warning signs when they come through private or unofficial channels.

## The safer rule

If money is being requested through a personal chat, suspicious link, or rushed message, do not treat it as legitimate.

Always go back to the official source and verify first.

## Why panic makes candidates vulnerable

Many people pay because they are afraid of missing the opportunity. That fear is what scammers use to rush applicants into bad decisions.

A calm verification habit protects you.
