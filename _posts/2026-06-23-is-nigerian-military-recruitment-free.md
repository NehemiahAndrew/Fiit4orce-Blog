Yes, genuine Nigerian military recruitment should be treated as free unless an official notice clearly states otherwise through the proper official channel. In practice, unsolicited payment demands are one of the clearest warning signs of a recruitment scam.

That is why candidates should be extremely careful when money enters the conversation.

## Why this question matters so much

Many scams target anxious applicants by pretending there is a fee for registration, shortlist placement, screening access, or final selection. These claims often sound urgent and convincing.

But pressure is exactly what scammers rely on.

## Common fake payment claims

Be careful if anyone says you must pay for:

- form access
- shortlist placement
- screening confirmation
- guaranteed success
- insider processing

These are major red flags when they do not come from a clear official process.

## The safer rule

If money is being requested through a private contact, unofficial link, or rushed conversation, do not treat it as legitimate.

Always go back to the official recruitment source and verify first.
