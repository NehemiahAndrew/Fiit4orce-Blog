To prepare for Nigeria Immigration screening without mistakes, begin before the final days. Many candidates wait until screening is close, then try to sort documents, fitness, and travel plans all at once. That usually creates pressure instead of readiness.

A steadier plan works better.

## What should Immigration candidates prepare first?

Start with the basics:

- application records
- identity and educational documents
- passport photographs
- a clear folder for originals and copies
- your route and timing plan

Even before the final checklist is released, this kind of early organization can reduce avoidable stress.

## Why does physical readiness still matter?

Screening is not only about paperwork. Candidates may need to move, wait, stay alert, and handle the day without becoming drained too early. You do not need an extreme routine. Walking, jogging, bodyweight drills, stretching, sleep, and hydration can already make a real difference.

Consistency helps more than panic.

## What mistakes should you avoid?

Avoid relying on unverified chat messages for screening instructions. Avoid late printing of documents. Avoid intense workouts right before the screening date. Avoid packing everything in a hurry on the same morning.

Calm preparation is part of good performance.
