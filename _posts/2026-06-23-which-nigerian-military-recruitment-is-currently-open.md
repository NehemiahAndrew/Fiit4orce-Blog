If you want to know which Nigerian military recruitment is currently open, the safest answer is the one published on the official recruitment portal of the service you want to join. Openings can change quickly, and old announcements often keep circulating long after a portal has closed.

That is why candidates should avoid depending on random social media posts, copied blog lists, or forwarded screenshots when checking whether recruitment is active.

## Why this question causes so much confusion

Military recruitment attracts intense attention, so outdated and unofficial information spreads fast. A candidate may see one post saying Army is open, another claiming Navy is about to start, and a third insisting Air Force registration has already begun.

The problem is that many of those messages are not current.

## The only safe way to check

To know which Nigerian military recruitment is open, you should check the official source for each service directly.

That usually means verifying:

- the Nigerian Army recruitment portal
- the Nigerian Navy recruitment portal
- the Nigerian Air Force careers portal

If a service has not published an official recruitment notice, do not assume recruitment is open just because people are talking about it.

## Why unofficial “open now” claims are risky

Some people post recruitment claims simply to attract traffic, while others share old announcements without checking dates. In worse cases, fake “open now” messages are used to lure candidates into scams.

Be careful when a message:

- does not point back to an official portal
- pressures you to act immediately
- asks for money
- shares a suspicious application link

If the source is unclear, do not trust the claim.

## What to do while waiting

Even if your target service is not currently open, you can still prepare productively.

Use the waiting period to:

- improve your fitness
- strengthen your study routine
- organize your documents
- keep monitoring official updates

That way, you are ready when the real notice appears.
