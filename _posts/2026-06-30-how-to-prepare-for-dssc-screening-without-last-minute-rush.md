To prepare for DSSC screening without last-minute rush, start early and treat screening like a process, not a one-day event. Graduate candidates often focus on academic credentials but still lose time because documents, fitness, and organization are handled too late.

Good screening prep begins before the final announcement feels urgent.

## What should DSSC candidates organize first?

Focus on:

- degree or HND documents
- NYSC or exemption details where applicable
- identity documents
- passport photographs
- professional licenses if needed
- a clear separation of originals and copies

The official DSSC notice should still be your final checklist, but early organization gives you breathing room.

## Does fitness still matter for DSSC applicants?

Yes. DSSC recruitment is not only about professional qualifications. Candidates still benefit from basic fitness, stamina, sleep discipline, and a body that is ready for demanding stages.

Steady fitness work is usually enough to start.

## What should you avoid?

Avoid leaving document review until the last moment. Avoid relying on unofficial document checklists without comparing them to the final notice. Avoid ignoring fitness because you assume graduate entry is only about paperwork.

Balanced preparation is safer.
