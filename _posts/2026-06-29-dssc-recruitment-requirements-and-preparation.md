DSSC recruitment is usually targeted at graduates and professionals, but candidates should not assume the requirements are the same every year. The current official notice should always decide eligibility, documents, age limits, and any professional qualification details.

Your job before applying is to prepare without guessing.

## What should DSSC candidates check?

Pay attention to:

- degree or professional qualification requirements
- age range for the current intake
- required documents and certificates
- NYSC or exemption details where applicable
- medical and physical fitness expectations
- application instructions and deadlines

Do not rely on old lists without comparing them with the current official notice.

## How should graduates prepare?

Start by organizing your academic records and professional documents. Check that names and dates are consistent. Build a simple fitness routine, because officer recruitment still requires discipline and readiness beyond paperwork.

Also practice aptitude-style questions and current affairs so you are not starting from zero when screening moves forward.

## What mistakes should you avoid?

Avoid assuming your degree alone is enough. Avoid ignoring fitness. Avoid waiting until the portal opens before locating important documents. Avoid private contacts who promise influence or guaranteed selection.
