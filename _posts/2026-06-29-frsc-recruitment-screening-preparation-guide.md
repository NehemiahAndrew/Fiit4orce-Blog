FRSC recruitment screening preparation should begin before the final screening date is close. Candidates who wait until the last minute often struggle with documents, travel plans, fitness, and confusing updates at the same time.

A calm plan is better than emergency preparation.

## What should FRSC candidates prepare first?

Start with the areas you can control:

- organize your documents
- monitor official FRSC updates
- build basic fitness
- practice aptitude-style questions
- plan your movement early if screening requires travel

These steps reduce stress and help you respond better when official instructions appear.

## How important is fitness?

Fitness matters because screening can involve movement, waiting, and physical checks. You do not need an extreme routine to start. Begin with walking, jogging, bodyweight exercises, stretching, and consistent sleep.

Steady preparation usually beats last-minute intensity.

## What mistakes should you avoid?

Avoid relying on unverified group-chat updates. Avoid assuming every viral post is current. Avoid packing documents the morning you need them. Also avoid overtraining right before screening, because fatigue can hurt your performance.
