The requirements for FRSC recruitment should always be confirmed from the current official notice. Even when a guide is helpful, it should never outrank the latest published instruction for your application cycle.

The smart move is to prepare early while staying flexible.

## What requirement areas do candidates usually look at?

Most candidates usually check:

- age eligibility
- education requirements
- citizenship and identity details
- physical and medical fitness
- document readiness
- any role-specific conditions in the current notice

The exact standard in each area can change, which is why the current official source matters.

## Why are old requirement lists risky?

Old posts and screenshots often mix information from multiple recruitment cycles. That can lead to wasted effort or wrong assumptions about eligibility. A requirement that applied before may not be the same now.

Treat unofficial summaries as background, not final authority.
