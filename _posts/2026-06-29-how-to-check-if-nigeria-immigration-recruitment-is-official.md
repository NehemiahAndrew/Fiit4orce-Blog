To check if Nigeria Immigration recruitment is official, verify every update from a real official source before you click, pay, or submit personal details. Recruitment scams often depend on urgency and appearance, not truth. A post can look convincing and still be fake.

Verification should come before action.

## What does official recruitment information usually look like?

Official information should point candidates to a recognized source, a public process, and instructions that do not depend on private negotiation. It should not pressure you into paying a stranger or using a suspicious form.

When reviewing a notice, ask:

- does it lead back to an official source?
- does it match the public process?
- is anyone asking for money or private help?
- does the link look suspicious?

If the message feels secretive or rushed, pause and verify.

## What are common fake recruitment warning signs?

Be careful if a message promises guaranteed selection, asks for shortcut payments, or tells you to act immediately without checking public guidance. These are classic scam patterns.

Pressure is not proof of authenticity.
