Yes, genuine Nigeria Police recruitment should be treated as free unless an official notice clearly states otherwise through the proper official channel. In most scam situations, fake payment demands are one of the strongest warning signs.

That is why candidates should become very cautious whenever money is mentioned.

## Why this question matters

Scammers often tell applicants to pay for form access, processing, shortlist placement, or screening. These demands are usually designed to create fear and urgency.

That is how they pressure candidates into bad decisions.

## Common fake payment traps

Be careful if anyone says you must pay for:

- registration access
- placement on a shortlist
- screening confirmation
- insider influence
- guaranteed selection

These are major red flags when they come from unofficial channels.

## The safer rule

If you receive a rushed payment request through personal chat, suspicious link, or private phone contact, do not treat it as legitimate.

Go back to the official source and verify first.
