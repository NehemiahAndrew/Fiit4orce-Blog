Nigeria Police physical screening preparation should focus on steady readiness, not last-minute panic. Candidates often think about documents and forget that screening can also test patience, alertness, movement, and basic physical condition.

Prepare your body and your records together.

## What should you train first?

Start with simple fitness habits:

- brisk walking or jogging
- push-ups or modified push-ups
- squats and lunges
- planks and core work
- stretching and mobility

The goal is consistency. You do not need to punish your body to improve. You need a routine you can repeat.

## What documents should you organize?

Prepare your application records, educational documents, identity documents, passport photographs, and any item requested by the current official notice. Keep originals and copies separate.

Always let the official Police recruitment instruction decide the final checklist.

## What should you avoid before screening?

Avoid extreme workouts a day before screening. Avoid poor sleep. Avoid relying on unofficial location or batch claims that you have not verified. Avoid packing documents in a hurry.

Simple discipline can prevent unnecessary problems.
